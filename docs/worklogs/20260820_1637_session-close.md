## The one blocker

**No BIOS and no disc images on this machine.**

Every visual milestone depends on it, and the QA harness is structurally but not
functionally verified because of it — it has never booted a real game. Resolving
this is the highest-value single action available, and it unblocks more than any
amount of further design work.

Everything else that could be done without it, has been.
